How Credora works

A merchant messages the WhatsApp agent "Hi" to get started, names their business, then just reports sales and expenses in plain language, in whatever way they'd normally describe it. Every sale gets a real PDF receipt back instantly, a verified record on chain, and /profit gives a net profit summary anytime.

"Sold 2 bags of rice for 15000 naira" "Bought fuel for 3000 naira" "Sold a laptop for 250k naira to Musa" "Sold shoes for 50 dollars" "Bought stock for 20k cedis"
1

Onboard once

The first message from any number triggers a single question, the business name. From then on the agent recognizes and greets that merchant by name.

2

Report sales and expenses

No forms, no app. An AI agent tells sales from expenses, extracts the amount and description, and flags anything unusual before it is recorded.

3

Get a real receipt

Every recorded sale sends a downloadable PDF receipt straight back into the same chat, and a permanent, verifiable record on chain.

4

Check net profit anytime

Sending /profit adds up revenue from chain and expenses tracked locally, for today, this week, this month, or all time. Bookkeeping only, it never touches the credit score.

5

Build a track record

Volume, receipt count, and selling streaks accumulate into a tier and a 0 to 1000 credit score, computed entirely from on-chain history.

6

Share it when it counts

A Credora ID is a one way hash, never a phone number, and a merchant hands it out deliberately when they want a lender to check their history.

7

Two audiences, one dashboard

This is a sales dashboard first. Merchants track their own growth here, and lenders use the exact same view to verify it, nothing is hidden either way.
